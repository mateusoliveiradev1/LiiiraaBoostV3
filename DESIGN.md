# Design System: Liiiraa Boost

## 1. Overview

**Creative North Star: "Pre-Dawn Flight Deck"**

The interface feels like a serious operator preparing a high-performance machine moments before a competitive session: ambient light is low, information is exact, and every illuminated signal has a reason to exist. It balances the monochromatic discipline and technical directness seen in FSOS-X with Hone's product polish and BravoBoost/StarBoost's sense of unlocked power, without reproducing any of their visual systems.

The experience is premium, trustworthy, and exclusive because it is controlled rather than ornamental. Density comes from useful evidence and progressive technical depth, never from filling space. Responsive motion confirms state, dependency, and consequence; it does not choreograph spectacle.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Near-monochromatic command environment with one rare cobalt signature
- Goal-first hierarchy with component-level technical depth
- Humanist technical typography with a dedicated monospaced data voice
- Responsive, low-latency motion tied to state changes
- Finished treatment for every ideal, degraded, risky, and recovery state
- Clear provenance for fixture, observed, measured, modeled, and unavailable information

## 2. Colors

**Strategy:** Restrained. Neutral graphite and near-black surfaces carry the product; cobalt/indigo appears on no more than 10% of a normal screen.

### Primary

- **Mineral Cobalt** `[to be resolved during implementation]`: Brand signature for the current focal action, verified selection, focus, and a small number of high-value interactive signals. It is never ambient decoration.

### Neutral

- **Instrument Black** `[to be resolved during implementation]`: Primary application environment with zero warm or cool tint.
- **Graphite Surface** `[to be resolved during implementation]`: Structural separation for working regions, navigation, inspectors, and raised operational surfaces.
- **High-Contrast Ink** `[to be resolved during implementation]`: Primary text and critical values, targeting at least 7:1 against the application background.
- **Muted Instrument Text** `[to be resolved during implementation]`: Secondary labels and metadata, maintaining at least 4.5:1 for normal text.

### Semantic

- **Verified, Warning, Critical, Experimental, and Unavailable** `[to be resolved during implementation]`: Independent semantic families that never borrow Mineral Cobalt's brand meaning and never rely on color alone.

**The Signal Rarity Rule.** Mineral Cobalt marks focus, agency, or verified selection. If a screen looks blue at a glance, the rule has been broken.

**The Neutral Surface Rule.** The dark environment remains perceptually neutral. Mood lives in the cobalt signature, typography, data, and motion—not in tinted black panels.

## 4. Elevation

The system is flat by default and separates depth through neutral tonal steps, spacing, occlusion, and deliberate dividers. Temporary surfaces such as command search, dialogs, menus, and recovery confirmations may lift above the workspace, but elevation is structural rather than decorative.

Responsive motion uses short, direct state transitions and ease-out curves. Layout properties are not animated when transforms, opacity, masks, or tonal transitions can communicate the same change. Every motion has a reduced-motion alternative.

**The Operational Depth Rule.** A surface may rise only when it temporarily interrupts or controls the layer beneath it. Permanent content does not receive ornamental floating-card shadows.

## 6. Do's & Don'ts

### Do

- **Do** make the current action, selected game profile, and system state readable before presenting raw telemetry.
- **Do** use Mineral Cobalt rarely enough that focus and verified selection remain unmistakable.
- **Do** explain risk, evidence, compatibility, previous state, and recovery in progressively disclosed layers.
- **Do** give loading, offline, permission, unsupported, partial-failure, restart, recovery, and expired-subscription states finished compositions.
- **Do** preserve complete keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, text scaling, and status labels in every surface.
- **Do** use measured data, source information, units, uncertainty, and provenance as visual material.
- **Do** keep application surfaces neutral and let identity come from composition, typography, signal color, and product behavior.

### Don't

- **Don't** resemble Advanced SystemCare: no dramatic fake issue counts, promotional pressure, upgrade banners, or miracle language.
- **Don't** create a generic gaming app covered in RGB, neon effects, decorative grids, diagonal stripes, or ornamental “gamer” motifs.
- **Don't** use a miracle-cleaner pattern with unverifiable percentages, invented urgency, or animated scans that pretend to measure performance.
- **Don't** produce a cold corporate dashboard without identity or a reskinned copy of Windows Settings.
- **Don't** make the interface excessively futuristic, theatrical, or difficult to operate.
- **Don't** place advertisements, cross-sells, plan promotions, or affiliate content inside operational flows.
- **Don't** ship placeholder copy, dead controls, fake final data, inconsistent states, or any surface that looks unfinished.
- **Don't** assemble the product from generic component-library cards, purple gradients, glass panels, giant metric tiles, or familiar SaaS dashboard templates.
- **Don't** over-round working surfaces or pair a thin border with a large soft shadow.
- **Don't** use color as the only carrier of verification, warning, risk, failure, or compatibility.

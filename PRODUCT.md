# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Liiiraa Boost serves demanding PC gamers, competitive players, streamers, PC technicians, builders, and hardware enthusiasts worldwide. Users range from people who need technical guidance to experts who want complete control; the interface must explain progressively without reducing the product to a simplistic one-click cleaner.

The primary surface is a Windows 10/11 Tauri desktop product rendered with web technology. A global web platform supports discovery, documentation, accounts, subscriptions, downloads, devices, support, and administration.

## Product Purpose

Liiiraa Boost exists to be the definitive PC optimizer: a deeply technical, hardware-aware system that improves real gaming performance and proves what changed. It diagnoses the current machine, prepares editable optimization plans, applies only compatible operations, activates game-specific profiles, measures results where reliable, and preserves a complete path back to the previous state.

Success means a user can see a measurable improvement adapted to their hardware, understand the evidence and risk behind it, and restore the system safely. The product must remain lightweight enough that the optimizer itself does not become a performance problem.

## Positioning

The definitive optimizer: measurable, hardware-specific gaming performance with complete control, evidence, and recovery.

## Brand Personality

Premium, trustworthy, and exclusive. The experience balances control, confidence, exclusivity, and power without letting any one quality become theatrical. Its voice is precise and calm under pressure: technically honest, never vague, never sensational, and never condescending.

BravoBoost/StarBoost is a reference for energy, ambition, and the feeling of unlocking performance. Hone is a reference for polish, hierarchy, and product confidence. Liiiraa Boost must combine the strongest intent of both while developing an unmistakably original system.

## Anti-references

The product must never resemble:

- A generic gaming app covered in RGB, neon effects, or decorative “gamer” motifs
- A miracle cleaner using invented problem counts, unverifiable percentages, or fake urgency
- A cold corporate dashboard without identity
- A reskinned copy of Windows Settings
- An excessively futuristic interface whose spectacle makes technical work harder
- A cheap ad-supported utility with promotions embedded in operational flows
- An unfinished product containing placeholder copy, dead controls, fake final data, inconsistent states, or visibly provisional surfaces
- A generic component-library dashboard assembled from stock cards, gradients, and familiar SaaS templates

## Design Principles

1. **Prove every claim.** Show measured facts, sources, compatibility, and uncertainty instead of marketing numbers.
2. **Make depth navigable.** Organize the primary experience around user goals and reveal component-level detail inside each domain.
3. **Keep the next action contextual.** The home surface prioritizes the next relevant action, current game profile, and system state rather than presenting a static wall of telemetry.
4. **Earn trust at risky moments.** Confirmation friction, explanations, snapshots, and recovery readiness increase in proportion to risk.
5. **Feel finished in every state.** Loading, empty, offline, partial failure, permission, recovery, expired subscription, unsupported hardware, and experimental states receive the same craft as ideal flows.
6. **Let the tool disappear into the task.** Motion, color, density, and technical detail support decisions and never exist merely to look advanced.
7. **Protect safety from monetization.** Transparency, history, alerts, and restoration remain available even when Premium access expires.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The product targets WCAG 2.2 AA from the first implementation. It supports complete keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen-reader semantics, scalable text and interface density, reduced motion, and status communication that never relies on color alone. Contrast is verified for all text, controls, charts, disabled states, and overlays. PT-BR and English ship first through an architecture designed for broader localization, longer text, locale-aware values, and right-to-left adaptation if added later.
